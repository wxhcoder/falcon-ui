## Natural Earth

`src/earth-points.ts` contains a generated, reduced land/ocean point set based
on Natural Earth v5.1.2 low-resolution land data. The generator records the
input SHA-256 hash in the generated file and does not download data at runtime.

Natural Earth map data is public domain and may be modified and distributed.
The source terms are available at
<https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>.

Source credit: Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @
naturalearthdata.com.
